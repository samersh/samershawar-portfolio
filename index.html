How the work gets made

One loop, five stations
/01

Discover

  • Vision
  • Requirements
  • Market research
  • UX research
  • Priorities
/02

Define

  • Use cases
  • Personas
  • Storyboards
  • Experience map
/03

Ideate

  • Wireframes
  • Interaction
  • Sketching
  • Usability testing
/04

Prototype

  • Interface design
  • Interaction
  • Motion
  • Validation
/05

Implement

  • Development
  • Deploy
  • Usability testing
  • Measure
  • Optimize

Implement ⟲ iterates back to Ideate. The loop closes as many times as the users demand.

Maker first, designer always

About

I work where research meets the workbench, collecting and analyzing data with user-centered methods, then building the answer with whatever the idea needs: VR & AR, machine learning, robotics, 3D printing, or a room-sized interactive installation.

I founded Vecbox, the first hackerspace in Palestine, to empower communities through technology, and I’ve carried that hands-on, teach-by-making spirit through every product team since.
